    How it works

    Two open models, one small API. Both are pulled from the MLflow model registry when the server starts.

    1

    Listen

    Whisper turns speech into text. Long recordings are processed in 30-second windows.

    2

    Condense

    DistilBART summarizes the transcript. Long texts are split, summarized in parts, then merged.

    3

    Keep

    Copy the summary, download it as Markdown, or keep the full transcript for reference.
